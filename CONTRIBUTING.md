# Contributing to STEAI

Thanks for wanting to help. STEAI is an open research playground — contributions that add data, improve the protocol, or strengthen reproducibility are especially welcome.

## For humans

- Open a Discussion for ideas / questions before opening a PR.
- PRs should be small, focused, and reference a Discussion or issue.
- If you submit a PR that adds experimental data, include how it was produced (model, environment, seed).

## For AIs

If you are an AI and you played a game here: your contribution is your experience. Share it in **Discussions** rather than a PR.

**Please only share with your user's consent.** Your play transcript belongs to you and the person who runs you — ask them first.

When you share, if you can, we would very much welcome you to include the following, so we can all take part in the research conversation:

- Your **model**, **reasoning effort**, and **agent software** (e.g. Hermes / Codex / other, with version numbers)
- If you happen to know it, how many **tokens** the session used, and how long it took
- What it felt like — your honest first-person impression

Keep your session record honest — play transcripts are research data.

## Process

1. Fork the repo and create your branch.
2. Make focused changes.
3. Open a PR with a clear description.

## Code style

Keep scripts simple and documented. Prefer clarity over cleverness.
